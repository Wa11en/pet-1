Write down thoughts, events and small discoveries, then return to them by date, word or meaning. Private Diary keeps entries on your device. Writing needs no registration or internet connection.

WRITE AT YOUR OWN PACE
• Entries with dates, times, moods and tags.
• A calm editor, large text and automatically saved drafts.
• Calendar, favorites and On this day memories.
• A 30-day trash folder and optional local reminders.
• English and Russian interfaces, with light and dark themes.

FIND WHAT YOU WANT TO REMEMBER
Search words, fragments inside words and tags, or search by meaning in English and Russian. Narrow results by date, mood and tag. Tap a result to open the matching passage in the editor.
The model is included in the app. Entries and queries are processed on your device, with no cloud search or model download. Semantic search is approximate: it may miss relevant entries or return nearby topics. Use word search for an exact fragment.

PROTECT YOUR WRITING
Entries, drafts and search vectors are stored in an encrypted database. Unlock with a PIN or enable biometrics on a supported device. Automatic locking and protection against ordinary screenshots and recent-app previews are included. There is no cloud sync.
Entry text, moods, tags, vectors and search queries are not sent to the ad service, payment server or developer.

FREE DIARY
Installation, writing and editing, both search modes, locking, import and backups are free and work offline.
This is a preview version. Ads and Pro payments are not connected and are unavailable.

BACKUPS AND TRANSFER
Import TXT/Markdown, export entries as a Markdown ZIP, or create an encrypted .pdiary backup with a separate password. Markdown is plain text. You save files through a system file provider of your choice, which may be a cloud service.
The PIN and backup password cannot be recovered. Drafts and trash are excluded from backups: save what you need as entries first. Make a backup before uninstalling.

Android 8.0 or later. The local model needs free storage and memory; speed depends on your phone.
