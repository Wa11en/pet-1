Do I need an account or internet?
No diary account is needed. Writing, search, locking, import and backups work offline. Internet is needed only for ad and Pro actions you choose. A file provider you select may also use a connection.

What is free?
Installation and all core diary features: entries, word and semantic search, locking, import, export and backups. Declining ads or letting Pro expire does not lock these features or delete entries.

What does Pro include and how do I renew?
No ads, plus on-device mood, frequent-tag and writing-regularity statistics. It costs RUB 99 per calendar month. Each further month is paid manually through SBP in a YooKassa payment form. There are no automatic debits. The amount and access period are shown before confirmation. Buying, checking and restoring Pro need a connection; confirmed access works offline until its stated expiry. Store your secret Pro recovery code separately; it does not transfer entries.

What is shared for ads and payments?
The ad SDK processes an IP address and technical device information, including technical telemetry. The payment service processes order and payment service data; YooKassa and the bank process payment details. Entry text, moods, tags, vectors and search queries are not sent to them. See the privacy policy for details.

Why did semantic search miss an entry?
Wait for the local index to finish, try different wording or use word search. Semantic search is approximate and may miss entries or return nearby topics. Both modes support date, mood and tag filters. Speed depends on the phone, entry count and available memory.

How do I protect and transfer entries?
Set up locking and optionally biometrics on a supported device. To transfer entries, create a .pdiary backup with a separate password and save it somewhere accessible. It contains saved entries, dates, moods, tags and favorites. Drafts, trash, the PIN, device key and settings are excluded. Restoring replaces current entries, clears drafts and trash, and rebuilds the index. Save important drafts and restore wanted trash entries first.

Can you recover a forgotten PIN or backup password?
The developer cannot recover the PIN, backup password or lost entries. If you can no longer unlock the diary or have lost the device key, only a previously created .pdiary with a known password can restore it after a clean installation. If biometric unlock still works, make a backup first. Uninstalling or clearing app data deletes local entries. Do not do this without a usable backup. Biometrics are optional and do not replace a backup.

Is Markdown encrypted?
No. A Markdown ZIP contains plain text. Use .pdiary for a protected portable backup. The system file provider you choose when saving may be a cloud service. Ordinary TXT/Markdown import does not restore all backup metadata and does not open ZIP files directly.

Can I restore a deleted entry?
Restore it from trash within 30 days unless it has been permanently deleted. Drafts and trash are excluded from export and backup. Restore wanted entries to the diary first.
